## Output Validation Summary

- **`icacls`**: Focus on whether the permission flag is **(F)/(M)/(W)** and whether the subject is **the current user or a group they belong to** (such as `Users`).
- **`accesschk`**: Focus on directly checking whether the output contains **`SERVICE_CHANGE_CONFIG`/`SERVICE_ALL_ACCESS`** (service configuration), or **`RW`** followed by the current user/`Users` (file write).
- **`whoami /priv`**: Focus on whether the privilege name appears and its state is **Enabled**.
- **Registry query**: Focus on whether the value is **`0x1`**.
